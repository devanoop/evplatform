# Rangewise brand and product design

## Name

**Rangewise**, chosen by Anoop on 2026-11-10. It names the thing EV buyers worry about most,
range, and promises sensible, honest advice about it. Plain English, easy to spell and to search.
The site launched as "Kilowise"; only the name and logo letter changed, the colours stayed.

Tagline: **Every EV in India. Honestly compared.**

## Where CarWale and CarDekho fall short for an EV buyer

1. **Lead capture first.** "Get offers", "Check on-road price" and EMI widgets ask for a phone
   number, which brings dealer calls. Rangewise never asks for one.
2. **Ads and sponsored placements** compete with the content. We show none, and no brand pays to rank.
3. **Built for petrol cars.** EVs get the same template, so the numbers that matter (real range,
   charge time, running cost) are buried or missing.
4. **Claimed range is presented as fact.** A MIDC P1 figure is shown next to a WLTP one. We show a
   real-world estimate adjusted for the test, with the claimed figure and test beside it.
5. **No answer to "is it right for me?"** We ask five questions and give three ranked matches with
   reasons and honest concerns (no home charging, battery rental pricing, optimistic test).
6. **Heavy, cluttered pages.** We use one static page, no tracking scripts, and a single primary
   action per screen.
7. **Sources hidden.** Every spec links to where it came from, and blanks mean the source didn't say.

## Design principles (inspired by how CRED launched, not copied)

* **Restraint**: a dark, calm base with one accent colour; generous space; few elements per screen.
* **Big confident numbers**: the figure you came for is the largest thing on the card.
* **One question per screen** in the match flow, answered with one tap.
* **Trust as a feature**: "0 ads, 0 phone numbers asked" sits in the hero.
* **Small delights**: smooth transitions, a fit-score ring, a compare tray that slides in.
  All turned off for people who prefer reduced motion.

## Visual identity

| Token | Dark (signature) | Light |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#0b0c0e` ink | `#f6f4ef` warm paper |
| Surface | `#141619` | `#ffffff` |
| Text | `#f2f0ea` ivory | `#121417` |
| Accent ("charge mint") | `#7df9c2` | `#0b9e6a` |
| Highlight ("gold") | `#e8c77b` | `#a87a12` |

* Type: **Sora** for display and numbers, **Inter** for text (Google Fonts, system fallback).
* Logo: a rounded mint square with a line-drawn "R".
* Car art: simple original side profiles per body type, tinted per brand. No manufacturer photos.
